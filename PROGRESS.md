## 2026-10-12: owner's decisions on tiers and the likely-clean tag (built, 115 tests pass)
- **Fourth outcome `LikelyClean`** (tag name to be set in settings, default `likely-clean`): clean evidence of at least 0.5 (lyrics with enough words and no scoring hits, or a "(Clean)" marker) and no explicit evidence. A playlist chooses its strictness: strict = only `clean`, balanced = `clean` + `likely-clean`, relaxed = everything except `explicit`. Below 0.5 (for example only a "(Radio Edit)" hint, now 0.4) the track is reviewed (reason WeakClean). Setting `LikelyClean` (0.5) moves the bar.
- **Word tiers (owner's list).** Severe (3): fuck and forms, cunt, the n-word and f-slur, cocksucker, shit and every compound of it (stem), bitch, dick, cock, pussy, whore, slut, tits, titty, blowjob, porn and their derivatives. Moderate (2): ass, asshole, piss (pissing, pisses), bastard, prick, twat, wanker, douche, jackass, dumbass, smartass, badass, goddamn and variants. **Mild (0, recorded but never scored): damn, hell, crap, arse, pissed.** The words are in `src/HallPass/Resources/default-wordlist.json` (the settings page will let the owner edit tiers and the allowlist).

Four LikelyClean tracks carry mild words that are listed in the evidence but not scored (damn x4, hell, damn, pissed x3).

## 2026-10-12: milestone 4 done: file tags and online lookups (plugin 0.4.0.0, 222 unit tests)
- **Core:** `EmbeddedTags` (Stage 1: the iTunes advisory, `rtng`, `explicit`, `contentadvisory` and similar fields become evidence; ISRC and album barcode are picked out; ffprobe JSON is read; the MP4 `rtng` atom is found in file bytes), `DeezerParser` / `ITunesParser` (answers read against recorded fixtures; Deezer's HTTP 200 errors, quota and "no data" told apart), `OnlineMatcher` (uses `TrackMatcher`: ISRC 0.95, title and artist and length 0.8 (+0.05 album), another length ignored; the explicit side wins ties and strong matches; **an online "not explicit" is capped at 0.85**), `TokenBucket` (rate limit), `OnlineCache` (answers kept 90 days, "nothing found" 14 days), and a new decision rule `CorroboratedClean`: two different sources saying clean (strongest 0.8 or more, 0.9 in Conservative) and nothing explicit make a track **clean**; one source alone stays likely clean. A lyric score of 10 or more is now near certain (0.95) so one catalog flag cannot overrule it.
- **Plugin:** `FileTagReader` (Emby's ffprobe, 30 s limit, no extra library), `OnlineStage` (Deezer by ISRC, else by artist and title when the ISRC lookup found nothing; iTunes by album barcode; rate limited per provider (Deezer 8 a second, iTunes 20 a minute), retries with backoff and `Retry-After`, a failure gives no evidence and is never "clean"; **opt-in** and it sends only artist, title, ISRC or album barcode), the cache saved as `HallPass.OnlineCache.json`, counters in the scan status ("asked / from saved answers / without a usable answer"), the settings (read file tags, online on or off, Deezer, iTunes, days kept) with a confirmation when turning online on, and the evidence version so changing any of these rescans.
- **Checked live on the test library (61 tracks, real Deezer and iTunes):** with online off, evidence from lyrics only; with it on, 65 questions asked and 32 answered from the cache inside the same scan (one question per album for iTunes), none failed; 33 tracks got Deezer evidence (ISRC matches read from the files by ffprobe) and 28 got iTunes evidence; decisions went from 7 explicit, 36 likely-clean, 18 review to **26 clean, 6 explicit, 12 likely-clean, 17 review**; a second scan asked nothing (97 from the cache), the saved answers survived an Emby restart, and switching online off made a scan that asked nothing and gave back the lyrics-only decisions. Disagreements went to review, for example "Caught in a Jar" (one "piss" in the lyrics, both catalogs say not explicit) and "The Season's Upon Us" (lyrics score 6, both catalogs say not explicit); "Kiss Me, I'm Shitfaced" (score 30) stays explicit although one weak Deezer match calls it clean.
- **Not verified:** the retry and back-off paths against a real quota or network failure (the parsing of those answers is unit-tested, the waiting and retrying is not); a large library (a thousand albums means about 50 minutes of iTunes questions at its rate limit; Deezer is much faster); the `rtng` atom and embedded advisory flags on real files (the test files had none); files whose ISRC or barcode is missing (those use the Deezer text search or get no online evidence).
- **Still temporary:** `/HallPass/Probe/Item` and `/HallPass/Probe/Tag`; remove before a release.
