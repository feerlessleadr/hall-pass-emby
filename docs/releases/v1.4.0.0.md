## Hall Pass 1.4.0.0

Hall Pass can now alert the Embidextrous app on your phone. It uses Emby itself, with no outside service, and every alert can be switched off on the plugin page.

### Phone alerts
- **Five alerts:** a scan finished, a scan did not finish, tags were written or put back, writing tags did not finish, and more tracks are waiting for your review.
- **No noise from quiet work.** A scan you start always reports. The nightly scan and the scan of new tracks report only when they looked at 25 or more tracks, and writing that happens by itself reports only when it changed 25 or more. A bulk import makes one summary, not one alert per batch. The review alert is sent only when the number of waiting tracks goes up, at most once every 10 minutes.
- **A job the server stopped in the middle of** is announced once after the restart, with a pointer to History where you can see and undo what it did.
- **How the app gets them:** the app subscribes on Emby's websocket (`HallPassEventsStart`) and Hall Pass pushes new alerts to that connection within about a second, with a heartbeat every 30 seconds. Only administrators can subscribe. This works even though the app keeps its sign-in token out of web addresses.
- **Missed an alert?** Hall Pass keeps the last 500 in a numbered list, so the app can catch up on what it missed. The numbers never go back, even after a restart or a damaged file.
- **Private.** No alert contains a file path, a track name, an artist or lyrics. Only administrators receive alerts.
- **Where to change it:** Settings > Phone alerts has a master switch and one switch per alert. The review alert is off by default, because it can come often after a big import. An alert that is switched off is not sent. The app decides which alerts you see, with what sound and at what hours.
- **For developers:** two new admin-only routes, `GET /HallPass/Events` and `GET /HallPass/Notifications/Catalog`, the websocket subscription messages `HallPassEventsStart`, `HallPassEventsStop` and `HallPassEvents`, and `GET /HallPass/Info` now also reports `ApiLevel` and `Features` (`events`, `catalog`). `ApiVersion` stays 1, so the app's Hall Pass tab keeps working as before. The details and sample messages are in `docs/NOTIFICATIONS.md`.

### Also
- New files in the plugin's configuration folder: `HallPass.Events.json`, `HallPass.Events.json.seq` and `HallPass.Notifications.json`. Removing the plugin does not need them.
- The Settings page has a sixth section, Phone alerts. Because the page changed, press Ctrl+F5 once after updating so your browser loads the new version.

### Known limits
- Alerts reach the phone only while the app is connected to your server; the app catches up on what it missed the next time it connects. Nothing is sent through any outside service.
- The scan and write alerts for a failed run use the sentence Emby gave, with every folder path removed.
- Checked on a test server with the Embidextrous side not yet built: the app was not part of this check.
