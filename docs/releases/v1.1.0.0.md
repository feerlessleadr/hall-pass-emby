## Hall Pass 1.1.0.0

Five additions, all optional or off by default.

### What's new
- **Edit the word list** (Settings > Word list): add words to the severe, moderate or mild tier, move a built-in word to another tier, stop built-in words from counting, and list words that are always fine. A box scores any text with your changes before you save. Changing the words makes the next scan look at every track again.
- **Lyrics fallback** (Settings > Online lookups, off): for tracks with no lyrics of their own, asks LRCLIB (lrclib.net) for them by artist, title, album and length. The text is scanned in memory and never kept. Because it matches by name its evidence is capped at 0.7: on its own it can make a track likely clean or send it to review, never certain.
- **Try it** tab: describe a track and see what Deezer, iTunes and LRCLIB say and what Hall Pass would decide. Contacts those services only when you press the button. Nothing is saved.
- **Rescan this track** button on every row of the review list.
- **Album and artist tags** (Settings > Writing tags, off): an album, and/or an artist, gets the explicit tag when any of its tracks is explicit, and loses it when none is. Written by Apply, shown in the preview, journalled and undone like track tags. New tracks found by the automatic scan can only add the tag to their album or artist.

### Limits
- The lyrics fallback depends on LRCLIB knowing the track, and it has been tried on a few tracks only. Wrong matches are guarded by the length check and the 0.7 cap, not eliminated.
- Roll-up tags use Emby's album and artist items. Tested on the small test library only; artists credited on several tracks are tagged if any of those tracks is explicit.
- Tested on Emby Server 4.10.1.0 with a small test library only.
