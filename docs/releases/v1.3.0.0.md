## Hall Pass 1.3.0.0

Fewer false alarms from words with an everyday meaning, and lyric sources that disagree now go to review. Also a wording and layout pass over the whole plugin page.

### Fewer false alarms
- **Each lyric source is judged on its own.** A track can have lyrics embedded in the audio file and a `.lrc` file next to it, and they are often different transcriptions. Hall Pass used to keep whichever scored higher, so an explicit word in the embedded text alone made the track explicit. Now each source gives its own evidence, and when one sounds explicit and the other clean the track is a **conflict for you to review**. Tap a track to see which source said what ("from the lyrics embedded in the audio file" or "from the file Song.lrc"). Sources that agree are shown as one line.
- **"Cocked" is not "cock".** Words like "cocked", "cocking" and "cocky" no longer match "cock" or "dick" (those two now count only as written).
- **"Dick" as a name.** "Dick" written with a capital in the middle of a line ("Moby Dick", "Dick Tracy") is read as a name and scores nothing; the evidence says "(a name)". At the start of a line, or in a line written in capitals, it still counts, because that could be either.
- **A single use of "dick" or "cock" is only a suspect** (the track goes to review), and two or more count in full.
- Because the evidence changed, the next "Scan now" looks at every track again.
- Where the words really are in a file, they stay: for example one lyric transcription of "Walk This Way" and one of "Hey Jude" contain words that the matching `.lrc` file does not (and the Hey Jude one transcribes Lennon's famous studio exclamation). Those tracks now show as conflicts rather than as explicit.

### Plainer wording and a tidier page
- Every explanation on the Dashboard, Review, History, Try it and Settings pages was rewritten as full sentences. The album and artist options read "Tag albums that contain an explicit track" and "Tag artists who have an explicit track".
- History shows one readable sentence per job, a wider date column, and "This job has since been undone."
- "Only tracks with a manual decision" is now "Only tracks I decided myself", explains itself, and sets Decision to All when ticked (the buttons say "Clear my decision"; the pill says "Your decision").
- Every tick box has its explanation underneath, in the same place on every page; the Settings cards are as wide as the other pages; "Ask Deezer" and "Ask iTunes" are indented under the online switch; the Albums and Artists lists have proper columns, say "3 of 16 tracks", and stack into blocks on a narrow window.

### Known limits
- The name check looks only at capitals. A lowercase "dick" in a name, or a name at the start of a line, still counts (as a suspect).
- A transcription that really contains a word will always be taken at its word: when the other source disagrees you get a review, not a verdict.
- Checked against your test files with the scanner and on a test server; wording and layout were checked in a desktop browser at wide and narrow widths.
