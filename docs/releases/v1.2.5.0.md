## Hall Pass 1.2.5.0

Plainer wording and tidier layout across the whole plugin page.

### What's changed
- **Clearer wording everywhere.** Every explanation on the Dashboard, Review, History, Try it and Settings pages was rewritten as full, plain sentences (no more sentences that trail off, such as "when any track is"). The album and artist options now read "Tag albums that contain an explicit track" and "Tag artists who have an explicit track". Review reasons read "No evidence", "Some sign of explicit content", "Sources disagree" and "Faint sign of clean".
- **History** is now readable: one sentence per job ("Changed 71 items; 3 items already had the right tags."), "This job has since been undone.", and a wider date column.
- **Review > "Only tracks I decided myself"** (it was "Only tracks with a manual decision") now explains itself: it shows just the tracks you marked explicit or clean by hand, so you can check them or clear your decision. Ticking it also sets Decision to All, because a track you decided no longer counts as needing review. The buttons and the pill now say "Clear my decision" and "Your decision".
- **Layout fixes.** Every tick box on every page now has its explanation underneath it, in the same place (before, some sat to the side). The settings cards are as wide as the other pages. "Ask Deezer" and "Ask iTunes" are indented under the main online switch. The Albums and Artists lists on the Review tab have proper column widths and say "3 of 16 tracks" instead of a bare number, and on a narrow window the Albums, Artists and History lists stack into readable blocks.

### Known limits
- Checked in a desktop browser against a small test library, at wide and narrow window widths.
