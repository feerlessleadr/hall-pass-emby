Keeping up to date

Hall Pass adds a scheduled task, Dashboard > Scheduled Tasks > Hall Pass > Scan music for explicit content, that runs every night at 3:30 and looks only at new or changed tracks. Change or remove its schedule there like any other task.
When Emby adds music, Hall Pass waits until things have been quiet for half a minute and then looks at the new tracks (so a big import is handled in small batches). It never runs at the same time as another Hall Pass job.
Off by default: then tags change only when you press "Apply decisions". When on, and dry run is off below, the nightly scan and the scan of new tracks also write their tags, and every change is journalled so you can undo it from History.

What Hall Pass looks at

Uses Emby's own ffprobe on this server. Finds an explicit/clean flag when a file has one, and the ISRC and album barcode that make online lookups precise. Nothing leaves the server.
Off by default. When on, Hall Pass asks Deezer (api.deezer.com) about a track by its ISRC, or by artist and title, and Apple iTunes (itunes.apple.com) about its album by barcode. It sends only the artist, title, ISRC or album barcode: never file names, paths, lyrics or anything about you or your server. Answers are kept on this server so nothing is asked twice, and a failure never makes a track look clean. Turn this off and nothing leaves your server.
iTunes allows about 20 questions a minute, so a first scan with iTunes is slow for a big library (one question per album). Deezer allows far more.
After changing these, press "Rescan everything" on the Dashboard so existing tracks are looked at again.
