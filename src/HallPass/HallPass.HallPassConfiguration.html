How to use Hall Pass

  1. Scan. Press "Scan now" below. This is safe while dry run is on: a scan only records what Hall Pass finds. It never changes your files.
  2. Review. Open the Review tab and go through "Needs review": mark tracks Explicit or Clean (or a whole album at once). Your choices are saved and a later scan never overwrites them. You can stop and come back, and you do not have to finish before step 5: tracks still in review simply get the "explicit-review" tag.
  3. Preview. Press "Preview changes" below to see exactly which tags would be written.
  4. Turn dry run off. Settings > Tags & writing > untick "Dry run", then Save. You do not need to scan again.
  5. Apply. Press "Apply decisions" below. Every change is journalled: History > Undo puts it back. Later, change some decisions and Apply again: only the differences are written.
Dry run is on. Scans and your decisions are recorded, but no tag is written to your library. To write tags: preview, then untick "Dry run" in Settings > Tags & writing, then Apply.

Your files

Uses Emby's own ffprobe on this server. Finds an explicit/clean flag when a file has one, and the ISRC and album barcode that make online lookups precise. Nothing leaves the server.

Online lookups

Off by default. When on, Hall Pass asks Deezer (api.deezer.com) about a track by its ISRC, or by artist and title, and Apple iTunes (itunes.apple.com) about its album by barcode. It sends only the artist, title, ISRC or album barcode: never file names, paths, lyrics or anything about you or your server. Answers are kept on this server so nothing is asked twice, and a failure never makes a track look clean. Turn this off and nothing leaves your server.
iTunes allows about 20 questions a minute, so a first scan with iTunes is slow for a big library (one question per album). Deezer allows far more.

Lyrics for tracks that have none

Off by default, and only used while online lookups above are on. Asks lrclib.net for the lyrics of tracks that have none of their own, sending only artist, title, album and length. The text is scanned in memory and never kept; only the verdict is. Because it matches by name it counts for less than the file's own lyrics: on its own it can make a track likely clean or send it to review, never certain.
After changing anything here, press "Rescan everything" on the Dashboard so existing tracks are looked at again.

Albums and artists

Off by default. An album with at least one explicit track gets the explicit tag when you press Apply, and loses it when none is explicit any more. Only the explicit tag is used for albums. Journalled and undoable like track tags.
Off by default. The same for artists (every artist credited on an explicit track).

Scheduled scan

Hall Pass adds a scheduled task, Dashboard > Scheduled Tasks > Hall Pass > Scan music for explicit content, that runs every night at 3:30 and looks only at new or changed tracks. Change or remove its schedule there like any other task.

New tracks

When Emby adds music, Hall Pass waits until things have been quiet for half a minute and then looks at the new tracks (so a big import is handled in small batches). It never runs at the same time as another Hall Pass job.
Off by default: then tags change only when you press "Apply decisions". When on, and dry run is off (Tags & writing), the nightly scan and the scan of new tracks also write their tags, and every change is journalled so you can undo it from History.
One Save covers every section. Saving re-works every decision from the stored evidence at once. Changing the skipped genres, the word list or the lookup options also needs a "Rescan everything" to take effect on tracks already scanned.
